Blender add-on · MMD (PMX / PMD) → Auto-Rig ProRig your MMD model with Auto-Rig Pro, in one click.

Import a model with mmd_tools, press one button, and get an Auto-Rig Pro rig. The add-on picks the Quick Rig mapping that fits the skeleton, and keeps the MMD helper bones (eyes, sleeves, twist bones) working.

Features

The MMD parts that Quick Rig alone does not handle

Quick Rig can rig almost any skeleton, but MMD models need a hand-made mapping and lose their helper bones after the conversion. This add-on does that work for you.

Skeleton detectionPicks the mapping for the spine, thumbs, D bones and twist bones of the model.
One-click conversionMapping, Quick Rig's Make Rig and the MMD fixes run together.
MMD append and IK keptEyes that follow the 両目 bone, sleeves chasing the arms, highlight bones still work.
Arm twist that matchesMMD and Auto-Rig Pro split the twist the other way around. The weights are swapped so the forearm twists smoothly.
Elbows that bend the right wayArms that are almost straight in the rest pose get their IK elbow pointed backward.
Readable controllersHair, skirt and accessory bones are drawn as lines along the bones, not a cloud of spheres.

The bone structures of 241 distributed MMD models were analyzed, and the conversion was checked on 36 representative models, one or more for each structure pattern.

Two modes

Replace the skeleton, or keep it

Not sure? Start with Convert.

Convert Default

Replaces the MMD skeleton with the Auto-Rig Pro skeleton. The weights move to the Auto-Rig Pro bones.

Best for: export to game engines, retargeting, the full Auto-Rig Pro feature set.

Preserve

Keeps the MMD skeleton, its append and its helper bones. Auto-Rig Pro only drives the MMD operation bones, the way a VMD motion does.

Best for: models with many helper bones that should deform exactly as in MMD.

Motion and IK / FK

Keep the VMD motion, take over with IK where you need it

With a VMD motion loaded, Convert bakes it onto the rig. The IK / FK panel then switches each arm and leg while keeping the pose.

  1. Bake the motionThe motion goes onto the FK controls. The IK-FK switches stay unkeyed.
  2. Switch a limb to IKPlace a hand on a table or a foot on a step. The VMD motion no longer moves that limb.
  3. Switch back to FKThe limb returns to the VMD motion exactly. With auto keying, the switch is keyed without blending.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Does it work without Auto-Rig Pro?

No. The conversion itself is done by Auto-Rig Pro: Quick Rig, which needs Auto-Rig Pro. Both are paid add-ons sold separately by their developer. This add-on is free and adds the MMD-specific part.

Will it work with my model?

Models with the common MMD skeleton (standard and semi-standard bones) are supported. The add-on detects the spine, thumb, D bone and twist bone layout and picks a matching mapping.

Some models drive their helper bones with their own systems that mmd_tools itself cannot reproduce. They do not deform correctly in either mode, and the panel warns about them.

Do I need to translate the bone names to English?

No. Keep the Japanese bone names and the default import settings of mmd_tools. VMD motions also load as they are.

What is not reproduced?
  • Physics (rigid bodies and joints).
  • In Convert, append whose parent is a bone mapped to Auto-Rig Pro (use Preserve for those models).
  • In Preserve, MMD IK that contains mapped bones, such as hair IK.
Am I allowed to convert a model?

This add-on grants no rights to models or motions. Whether you may modify a model, use it in other software or publish what you make is decided by its terms of use. Check them before you convert.

Something went wrong. What now?

See the troubleshooting section of the README. If that does not help, please report it in GitHub Issues. Do not attach model or motion files; describe the bone names and the hierarchy instead.
